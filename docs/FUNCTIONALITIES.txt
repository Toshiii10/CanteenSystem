GR 6: CANTEEN SALES AND INVENTORY SYSTEM - IMPLEMENTED MODULES

ADMIN / STAFF PORTAL
1. Authentication and role routing
2. Dashboard counters for menu, orders, payments, low stock, expiry and sales
3. POS cash sales with recipe-based ingredient deduction
4. Online order confirmation, preparation, ready, completion and rejection workflow
5. Payment verification/rejection with automatic balance recalculation trigger
6. Customer and employee account management, activation and password reset
7. Menu categories and products CRUD/Search
8. Ingredient inventory, recipes, suppliers, batches, purchasing and receiving
9. Stock movements and inventory adjustment tracking
10. Promotions, expenses, announcements, feedback, messages, notifications and audit logs
11. Reporting views and CSV export

CUSTOMER / USER PORTAL
1. Self-registration, login, profile update and password change
2. Browse/search available menu and create multi-item pickup orders
3. View order history and itemized purchase history
4. Cancel eligible unpaid pending/confirmed orders
5. Submit e-payment references/proof for administrator verification
6. View announcements and notifications
7. Send support messages to administration
8. Submit feedback only for the user's own completed orders

- 24 tables
